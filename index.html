Business Ownership Rules & Regulations

To maintain a fair, balanced and immersive economy, all business owners are expected to follow the rules outlined below.

Business Ownership

  • 48 hours of playtime on the SRC server are required before a business can be owned.
  • Each player may own one (1) business per account, regardless of the number of characters they possess.
  • Businesses may not be transferred between alternate characters on the same account to bypass this rule.

Payment Requirements

  • Business owners are responsible for making all required business payments on time and in full.
  • If you are unable to make a scheduled payment, communicate with County Administration as soon as possible.
  • Two consecutive missed payments without communication will place the business in default.
  • Businesses in default may be repossessed by the Saddle Ridge County Government and returned to the market.
  • Payment concerns may be reviewed case-by-case when communicated in advance.
  • Repeated late or missed payments may result in a review of business ownership.

Business Activity & Repossession

  • Business owners are expected to remain reasonably active and involved in the operation of their business.
  • If there is no business activity and no communication with County Administration for 14 consecutive days, the business will be considered abandoned.
  • Abandoned businesses may be repossessed and placed back on the market.
  • If you know you will be away or unable to operate your business for an extended period, communicate with County Administration.
  • Communication regarding an absence may prevent repossession; however, extended absences may still be reviewed case-by-case.
  • Businesses should not be held indefinitely by inactive owners when other citizens are interested in actively operating them.

Hiring Employees

  • Business owners are encouraged to hire other players to help operate their business.
  • Employees help create immersive roleplay, provide customer service and ensure businesses remain active even when the owner is unavailable.
  • Business owners are responsible for managing and supervising their staff.

Products & Merchandise

  • Businesses may sell goods and products crafted by the business, with the exception of General Store owners.
  • Businesses may sell raw materials and supplies directly related to the operation of that business.

Selling a Business

  • Business owners may choose to sell their business back to the County.
  • Businesses sold back to Saddle Ridge County will receive 50% of the original purchase price.
  • Improvements, inventory and customizations are not included in reimbursement unless otherwise approved.
  • Business owners may not privately sell their business to another player.
  • All business sales must go through the Saddle Ridge County Real Estate Office.
  • A business owner may hand their business over to another player at no cost.

Whitelisted Families & Gangs

  • Approved whitelisted families and gangs may collectively own one (1) business of each business category.
  • They may collectively own one (1) ranch.
  • Ownership limits apply to the organization as a whole, not individual members.
  • County Administration reserves the right to deny or revoke ownership if these limits are abused or used to monopolize the County economy.

Standard Business Assets

  • Storefront
  • Secure business storage
  • Business-specific crafting table
  • Business ledger for financial management
  • Additional equipment, upgrades, decorations or specialty crafting stations may be available depending on the type of business or future County expansions.
Communication is Key

Circumstances happen. If you are having trouble making payments, remaining active, or operating your business, communicate with County Administration before the issue becomes a default or repossession matter.
