Before You Purchase

Eligibility: You must have been in the county for at least 48 hours of playtime, meet the profession-training requirements, and receive approval from the SRC Business Real Estate Office. Each character may own one business at a time, following the county multijob guidelines.

Profession Training — Required

  • Before purchasing, work with an existing business in your chosen profession for one week.
  • Complete a minimum of 8 hours of work in that profession during the training week.
  • The supervising business owner must provide proof or verification of completed training to the Business Real Estate Office.
  • Training and verification must be completed before purchase approval.
  • If you cannot find a business to train under, open a ticket and communicate with the office so alternatives can be discussed.

Business Rules & Regulations

All business owners and prospective buyers are responsible for understanding and following county policies.

01 · Business Ownership & Activity

  • Each character may own one business at a time, following the county multijob guidelines.
  • Businesses must remain active and operated in good faith.
  • 7 consecutive days of inactivity without communication may result in repossession.
  • If you will be away for more than a week, submit a support ticket to the Real Estate Office.
  • Business taxes are 10% of the business cost every 30 days, automatically deducted from the tax ledger; insufficient funds may result in the business being locked.
  • Business loans are not offered.

02 · Communication & Abandonment

Real life comes first. If you are taking a break, cannot operate your business, or are leaving the county, communicate with the Business Real Estate Office through a support ticket.

Permanent ownership ineligibility: An owner who abandons a business without communication and has it repossessed as a result will no longer be eligible to own a business in Saddle Ridge County. This is separate from the 90-day restriction for voluntarily relinquishing a third business.

03 · Products & Merchandise

  • Businesses may sell goods crafted by their own profession.
  • Businesses may sell raw materials and supplies directly related to their business operations.
  • General Stores have additional approved merchandise options, subject to the restrictions below.
  • All businesses may sell Trapper trade goods, except the tanning rack, which may only be sold by General Stores.
  • Do not buy large quantities of another owner's products to resell at substantially higher or lower prices. Unauthorized resale of this kind violates county business rules.

Purpose: Protect our player-driven economy and preserve a meaningful role for every profession.

04 · General Store Rules

  • General Stores may sell their approved crafted and merchandise items.
  • No guns or gun parts. These belong to Gunsmiths.
  • General Stores may arrange to sell products from other businesses with the original owner's permission and consent, subject to profession restrictions.
  • Products exclusive to Saloons, Cafés, Bakeries, and Butchers may not be resold by General Stores.

Support your local General Stores and the other hardworking business owners across the county.

05 · Selling Back to the County

  • Owners may sell their business back to Saddle Ridge County for 50% of the original purchase price.
  • Improvements, inventory, and customizations are excluded from reimbursement unless otherwise approved.
  • Before the buyback process starts, remove all money from your business ledger and take everything you wish to keep out of business storage. Any items or money left behind will be lost and cannot be recovered.

06 · Private Business Sales & Transfers

  • Owners may privately sell a business to another player.
  • The price of the business itself cannot exceed its current official listing price.
  • Inventory may be sold separately for an additional agreed-upon amount.
  • Every sale must go through the SRC Business Real Estate Office.
  • The new owner must be approved by the office and meet applicable ownership requirements.
  • The selling owner must hire the prospective buyer and allow them to work at the business for at least 8 hours before the transfer. These hours can count toward the one-week profession-training requirement if properly completed and verified.
  • An SRC representative must be present on the day of the sale to oversee and finalize the transfer.
  • No private handover or agreement is official until approved and recorded by the office.

07 · Changing Businesses & the Three-Business Rule

  • New owners must remain at their establishment for at least two weeks before requesting relocation or a change of business.
  • Business changes require office approval; the county buyback remains 50% of the original purchase price and the new business is purchased at full price.
  • If a player has owned three different businesses and voluntarily given each one up, they are ineligible to own another business for 90 days.
  • After 90 days, a new application is subject to all current eligibility and training requirements.

08 · Property & Ranch Reminders

  • Properties are first come, first served; have funds available when applying.
  • Property reservations are not accepted.
  • Ranch add-ons cost 35% of the property cost and require a ranch-suitable property and approval.
  • Ranches are considered businesses and are subject to applicable business ownership rules, including the one-business-per-character limit and multijob guidelines.
